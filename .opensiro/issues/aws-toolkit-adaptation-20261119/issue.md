**Expected user benefit:** HYPOTHETICAL earlier recognition and safer validation of one future compatibility change with a verifiable later-run outcome. Real avoided failures, user demand and engineering savings are NOT_OBSERVED / NOT_MEASURED.

**Architectural sufficiency argument:** Existing Konductor source supplies the input surface (manifest + `doctor`), cited external-research capability, legitimate S3 orchestrator and normal specialist execution.
